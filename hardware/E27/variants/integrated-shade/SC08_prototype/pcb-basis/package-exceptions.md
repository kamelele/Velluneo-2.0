# Full native SC08 package inventory supplement

`package_inventory.py` parsed all 47 BOM-selected local footprints, all 112 numbered copper pads and every 102 intra-footprint pad pair, including eight same-net pairs. No package was omitted. The one unused comparator output U2.1 is provisionally a local control-domain pin, not silently dropped. Exact planar roundrect/oval/circle/rect distances are calculated; U3's custom cathode/anode pad geometry is processed as the union of its actual rectangular anchor and filled rectangular primitive. This is a copper-gap inventory, not assembled component-surface creepage.

The complete reproducible inventory is `all-package-pairs.json`. `package-exception-candidates.json` contains the 10 affected references and every pair falling below the proposed clearance or surface reservation. The other 37 references have no intra-package candidate under this matrix; this does not qualify their voltage ratings, mounting, routing, solder fillets or component-body insulation.

## Candidate exceptions requiring explicit disposition

* **BR1 MB10S-13:** DC_RETURN3–AC_N4 and AC_FUSED2–BUS_PLUS1 each 1.740 mm. Candidate nominal drawn-copper floor 1.70 mm for those exact package pairs only. Rectifier package rating and current/surge qualification do not independently prove board or package-surface insulation.
* **U1 LYT0006D-TL:** BP1–DRAIN4 3.210 mm; FB2–DRAIN4 1.940 mm; DRAIN4–SOURCE5 3.000 mm; DRAIN4–SOURCE6 3.139608 mm; DRAIN4–SOURCE7 3.688433 mm. Candidate exact-pair drawn floors respectively 3.20, 1.90, 2.95, 3.10 and 3.65 mm. The first/last miss only the 4 mm surface reservation; the others also miss the 3.2 mm air target. U1.8 has no additional missed pair. These values do not authorize a 1.9 mm board-wide drain rule.
* **D1 C4D02120E:** cathode1–anode2 3.360 mm, CASE–anode2 2.000 mm. Candidate nominal drawn floors 3.30 and 1.95 mm for these exact pairs. Actual lead/package path and high-frequency repetitive voltage remain part of qualification.
* **L2 and L3 RFC1010B-155KE:** terminal gap 3.500 mm each; candidate nominal drawn floor 3.45 mm. They meet the 3.2 mm air target but not the 4 mm surface reservation. The winding insulation/terminal-body path and actual differential waveforms are not established by saturation/current data.
* **R13 and R14 HP1206 divider resistors:** each 1.800 mm terminal gap; candidate nominal drawn floor 1.75 mm. R13 bridges COUT_PLUS to OV_MID; R14 bridges OV_MID to OV_SENSE. Both are intentional output-domain voltage drops. They need explicit component-contained functional exceptions, exact voltage/power rating checks and one-open/short fault assessment. They are not ≤15 V circuitry merely because the final divider output is 0.4 V.
* **U4 and U5 AQY221R2VY:** same-height input/output pairs 1–4 and 2–3 are 3.450 mm; diagonal input/output pairs 1–3 and 2–4 are 3.496770 mm. Candidate nominal drawn floor 3.40 mm for all four exact input–output pairs on each relay. The 4 mm surface reservation is unmet; the 3.2 mm clearance target is met. Local same-side input/LED and output/BP pairs use the declared low-differential rule and meet it. Both sides remain mains referenced.
* **D4 STTH110A:** nominal copper gap 2.630 mm, candidate exact-pair drawn floor **2.60 mm**. D4's reverse-differential calculation remains unresolved. Keep this listed as an explicit functional HV package exception; do not invent a low-voltage result or waive all BUS_PLUS–DRAIN_BLOCKED routing elsewhere. ST's exact SMA land-pattern source is already pinned in the BOM; voltage rating alone does not establish a particular creepage path.

The proposed floors are rounded-below nominal CAD geometry to provide usable rule thresholds; they are not a fabrication-tolerance analysis, approved safety distances or physically verified minimum assembled distances. Obtain actual copper/etch/solder tolerances when evaluating them. An assembled lead or solder fillet may extend beyond copper. If these floors become accepted experimental constraints, their authority must explicitly identify SC08, exact component/ref/pin pair and laboratory scope. Product certification remains open.

## Enforcement and stage disposition

The proposed native fabrication floor of 0.30 mm plus a named AC/BUS class at 1.50 mm is only a coarse lower bound. It **does not enforce** HV 3.2/4 mm or output 2 mm targets; do not report native DRC as complete insulation validation. The pinned Toolkit cannot be claimed to migrate unsupported custom pair rules automatically.

A separate reproducible voltage-pair checker should inspect the final board's pads, tracks, vias, zones and both sides against the exact domain matrix. The current script only inventories unplaced package pads. Checker exceptions must match `(component reference, physical pin pair, local geometry)`; matching two net names globally is unsafe because it would exempt remote routing on the same nets. Intentional local escape traces need a reviewed finite polygon per component, not an arbitrary whole-board exception. Same-layer nearest-copper distance is not actual surface creepage around a package/hole/slot; retain manual surface-path inspection and later physical test evidence. Across-layer overlap is a separate solid-insulation assessment, not an automatically passing air gap.

For **unpowered experimental bare-board fabrication**, lack of physical test results is not a contradiction: the board is needed to obtain them. What blocks approval now is the absence of a real, scoped decision accepting each deliberate package exception and defining the board/fixture constraints. Resolve that with a clear owner-approved laboratory requirement crosswalk and technical disposition, not a fictitious standards pass. The disposition may accept these component-contained functional gaps as explicitly unqualified experimental features, with a guarded qualification plan and no energization/product-release acceptance inferred. If the owner declines that experimental uncertainty, change packages/geometry before ordering.

Before any mains energization, require a qualified test owner to accept the actual assembled fixture, instruments, stop limits, voltage/ripple/thermal/fault/surge sequence and insulation verification. No package exception automatically authorizes mains testing. Preserve original 17 requirements and failure records as future energized/system obligations; do not delete them to pass a bare-board checker.
