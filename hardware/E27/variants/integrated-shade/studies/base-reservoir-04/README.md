# Base-reservoir preference, BR04

The owner prefers the capacitor in the base if this does not materially complicate the design. Treat this as a conditional product preference, not approval of the unfinished circuit. The active investigation now keeps COUT68uF/160V and its discharge resistors in the base; the detachable LED module can remain simpler. The previous module-reservoir architecture and its size experiment are retained as historical comparisons.

The existing CS03 all-base reservation experiment was64x40mm with a20mm-tall capacitor, before addingPCB and enclosure clearances. It did not include an independently sized additional reconnect-protection mechanism. This preference does not establish those dimensions as a manufacturable board, an optimum or fit in an E27base. No new CAD or part qualification is claimed.

AN60Fig8 already provides a discharge path from the output reservoir through L2, D2, R6 and the latched SCR. The new ideal passiveRLC screening quantifies what happens only AFTER a successful trip, with no continued energy from the converter. It cannot establish protection timing or guarantee a safe reconnect. At the110V/+20%C scenario the reservoir stores0.49368J, which must be handled by the discharge network; a nominal0.75W resistor rating alone cannot qualify that pulse. ExistingR6 remains a candidate until its pulse/thermal capability is checked, or it is replaced and the size study updated.

Engineering consequence: base placement is a viable direction to investigate and does not by itself require reverting to an isolated supply. It removes the module capacitor burden, while keeping the stored-energy/reconnect problem inside the base design. Prefer reusing the documented latch/discharge mechanism if actual thresholds,timing and pulse qualification support it. Add an output-disconnect/current-limiting function only if the required fault envelope cannot otherwise be met; do not assert in advance that no extra parts are needed.

All original17requirements and CS03 blockers remain controlling. Process choice, exact protection/circuit/parts qualification and schematic/spatial human gates remain open. No manufacturing, mains test or hotplug operation is authorized. The current task records the preference and a narrow calculation; it does not advance an engineering stage.
