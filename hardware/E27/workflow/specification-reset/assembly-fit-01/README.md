# ASSEMBLY-FIT-01 — complete lamp envelope and cutaway

The owner requested the next mechanical step and a preview in the chat. This packet keeps the **rectangular 32 × 60 mm longitudinal power driver**, round LED insert and whole-lamp target **diameter 60 mm / full height 120 mm**. It adds a reproducible all-part envelope model and a computed cutaway. It is not a finished lamp, native product PCB, routed layout or safe-operation qualification.

The conditional stack is **118.5 mm nominal**, or **119.8 mm** after summing the declared required positive tolerances. The allocated finished maximum diameter is **59.7 mm**. Those tolerances, cap dimensions, insulating material and custom cooler are design/procurement requirements, not confirmed supplier or manufactured values. The calculations establish feasibility only if those inputs are met. There is no guarantee that currently unselected parts meet them.

Thus width and populated thickness, rather than the board-plan diagonal, constrain its fit in the housing. The current analytical layout moves C1 inward for a full-face vent reservation, rotates/moves F1, and adjusts T1/R1 to remove direct opposed R1/C1 body projection. layout-delta.json records every position change against RECT-01. These are **unapplied proposal positions**; active electrical CAD, approved graph, parts and native libraries remain unchanged.

All 31 positions/79 pads still fit the native rectangular courtyards, with no listed same-face or opposite-face THT joint collisions under the declared working models. Body/installed-height envelopes, individual position tolerance and correlated board mounting errors are additionally checked in 3D against the lower circular and upper rounded rectangular cup cavity. Full native Fab graphical bounds include circles, not merely their endpoints. C1 uses the source maximum diameter; T1 uses the explicitly unverified finished construction box. Through-hole tails use an explicit unqualified 3 mm normal trim/solder bound. Joint bodies, body pairs, removable end-cradle envelopes and the full-face capacitor vent reserve are checked without silently zeroing wire/assembly height.

Under these allocations, minimum component/cup clearance is **0.55 mm** at T1, and the reserved 2 mm C1 end-face vent space retains **0.48 mm** additional worst-case cavity clearance. Nominal guide dimensions admit the stated PCB tolerances; retention force and process capability are not established. This is not a normative creepage, insulation, joint-standard or actual assembly certificate.

## Physical construction proposal

The initial two-piece lap at Z34–40 blocked insertion and was rejected in independent Reality finding RF-01. The corrected construction uses an **upper cup with a wide open mouth at Z40**, a **separate joining sleeve at Z34–42**, a lower flared nose and a removable lid. No narrowing sleeve, nose or end cradle is present while the populated PCB is loaded. The sleeve is fitted afterwards; its largest outer radius fits the upper cup mouth. Its narrow section only surrounds the lower PCB, below C1/T1. The lower nose then slides over the sleeve, and end cradles are fitted with nose/lid. The sleeve has a 6 mm lower overlap and a 2 mm upper overlap; neither is a qualified electrical seam. The radial lap stack is now 3.55 mm (two 1.5 mm walls plus a 0.55 mm assembly gap). `assembly-path.json` records the phase-wise conservative sweep and cradle checks. Actual wires, tooling, retention forces and material/seam qualification remain open. This extra sleeve is a mechanical proposal, not a changed electrical BOM or a manufactured part. The lower nose surrounds the E27 connection and lower components. Its upper cavity becomes a rounded rectangular capsule around the transformer. The 2 mm upper overlap is a mechanical allocation, not a claim of sufficient mains-insulation creepage. A lid service passage, stepped insulating bushing and panel cutout reserve the selected-interface space; actual material and contact selection stay open. A **custom annular fin structure** surrounds the upper capsule, keeping the long driver inside the radiator's height instead of stacking it below a solid cooler. The proposed metal has a source-model minimum web of 1.12 mm after declared geometric tolerances; machining, structural retention and thermal performance remain unqualified.

A 2 mm heat plate, 0.1 mm TIM and 1.5 mm round 48 mm MCPCB support the LED-ring proposal. An offset central passage and insulating bushing reserve space for an unselected two-contact service interface. The renderer shows a reserved volume, not an existing connector or working mating mechanism. Actual wire bends, strain relief, material/insulation, contact force/stroke and replacement cycles still need design and selection. The round MCPCB's offset central opening is also a new mechanical proposal, not a captured panel PCB. The diffuser and latch interface are allocated, not optically verified.

No stock MPS60 or earlier horizontal-disc **5.35 K/W** cooling result is transferred to this custom annular structure. It needs its own thermal model and validation. Removing direct opposed resistor/capacitor body projection does not prove capacitor life, NTC response or safe fault temperature. Vent gas escape and enclosure pressure behavior are not established by reserving headroom.

## Source conflict retained honestly

Root obtained and visually inspected the actual public [Rubycon cautions PDF](https://www.rubycon.co.jp/wp-content/uploads/catalog/al-cautions.pdf), SHA256 941413a32d7240ec38ac4c68f1f0d2eba2c85dcacce080860461693e796ce7f7, page2. Its table labels the D6.3–16 mm space as **2 mm MAX**, while manufacturer-indexed text says MIN and the surrounding prose calls for sufficient room for vent opening. This discrepancy is not silently repaired. The model imposes a **project 2 mm full-face free-space reservation**, pending manufacturer clarification; it does not claim a newly verified manufacturer's minimum or a safety pass. The PDF also warns that the sleeve is not a guaranteed insulator, which the model does not rely on.
