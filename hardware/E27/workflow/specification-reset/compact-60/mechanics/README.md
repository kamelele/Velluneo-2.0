## Thermal design frontier

LED spectral analysis replaces the old8W assumption with2.636-3.398W typical panel+diffuser heat. The driver scenario model gives1.336-2.156W, not a guaranteed upper bound. A no-optical-credit LED rectangle remains7.808W; it cannot subtract750lm at40C because the accepted luminous requirement is at25C and source spectra are typical.

Malico's official LED page lists MPS60-30 natural-convection5.35K/W and30mm height. Treating this as a screening input, with40C ambient and20% of2.156W driver heat coupled into it, gives84.08C at the panel base. The proposed85C base condition derives from retained105C solder/125C junction ceilings using1K/W common+10K/W local+15K/W junction screening allowances. Those allowances are not qualified MCPCB/LED maxima. At25% worseRth the base exceeds95C, so this does not close orientation/enclosure tolerance. The20mm-high6.08K/W source option exceeds90C even before that penalty. Exact source drawing, tolerance, airflow/orientation/testpower, MCPCB stack/spreading/TIM/preload/replacement-cycle behavior and primary-cup model are required. The cooler is a candidate, not a selected qualified component; nominal60mm leaves no diameter allowance for guards/tolerance.

The prior68/75C nodes and4W/8W budgets were agent proposals, not accepted product limits. They may be re-derived honestly; actual safety/life/ambient/fault limits must not be weakened. The120mm proposal is geometrically promising but **not ready for PCB entry**. Genuine outstanding conditions appear in thermal-frontier.json and the packet closure ledger.
