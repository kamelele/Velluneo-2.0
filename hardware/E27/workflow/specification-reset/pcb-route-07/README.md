# PCB-ROUTE-07 — actual routing development and process comparison

This packet keeps the owner-approved 33-part electrical basis (81 pads, 23 functional nets and four explicit NC singletons). It contains two separate native candidates. **The four-layer process has not been approved or adopted. Neither board is accepted for manufacturing or powered testing.**

Open `studies/sense/Velluneo_route07.kicad_pro` for the two-layer candidate: 515 track segments, 26 vias, native zero violations, five unconnected items and zero schematic parity findings. The missing branches belong to DRAIN and BULK_POS; do not mistake this for completed two-layer routing. The unchanged `design/` 41 x 60 mm baseline remains an independent historical input, with no tracks and 54 unconnected items.

Open `studies/four-layer-comparison/Velluneo_four_layer_COMPARISON.kicad_pro` for the separate unadopted comparison: 643 track segments, 19 vias, native **zero violations, zero unconnected items and zero schematic parity findings**. All physical pin memberships match the independently selected intent. This establishes geometric connectivity only. The board's generic four-layer representation is not an approved dielectric/copper stackup or a quote from a manufacturer.

Both trial boards are rectangular 43 x 60 mm, widened 1 mm per side from the baseline. Three declared placement changes (F1 rotation, R4 backside location, R10 rectifier-local front location) retain all parts, values and pin assignments. See `layout-alternative.json`. The whole-lamp target stays diameter60 x fullheight120 mm. The two-layer process is retained as the primary development branch; do not select a four-layer manufacturing process from this comparison without the owner's decision.

The R10/D2 damping connection decreases from about 50.26 mm to 2.41 mm. C3's negative, R3 and R15 return to the auxiliary branch; they do not share the local IC return. The IC/bulk trunk improves from C06's 38.695 mm to 27.033 mm in the two-layer study and about 23.581 mm in the comparison. The 2.7 mm, 1 mm-wide R9-to-C1 power strap remains shared impedance: this is not a complete negative Kelvin connection. Native diagnostic cuts outside the 1.25 mm C1 junction neighborhood keep the IC, shunt, bridge and auxiliary trees separate; cuts at R9.1, Q1.3 and T1.4 also distinguish their intended branches. These are topology checks, not impedance or noise measurements.

The comparison contains real power paths: T1.5–Q1.2 36.999 mm, Q1.2–D1.2 25.475 mm, C1.1–T1.1 20.494 mm, T1.1–R16.1 13.834 mm and C1.1–BR1.1 33.976 mm, each planned at 0.4 mm width. These are open branch lengths, not validated closed-loop area, inductance or current ratings. **DRV remains long at approximately 43.1 mm and is a major open layout/performance finding.** The local U1/C2 VDD bypass is protected at approximately 1.58 mm; the U1/C2 ground branch is separately checked. Full current, driver-loop ringing, reference bounce, CS spikes and EMI/error margin are unqualified.

The original six-segment SENSE branch (25.197855 mm) and CS branch (1.931321 mm) remain intact. An early inner-layer trial approached them by only 0.130 mm in projection and was rejected. The final comparison reserves a projected HV exclusion corridor around the measurement conductors on every copper layer. Fresh sampled all-different-layer HV-track edge gaps are at least 3.381 mm for SENSE and 5.165 mm for CS, as recorded in `design/checks/saved-geometry.json`. This conservative routing screen is additional to unchanged native numeric rules; it does not establish dielectric isolation, electromagnetic fields or measured sensing accuracy. Other quiet-net coupling remains open.

Fresh native evidence is under `design/checks/`; saved-board topology and actual track exports are in each study's `route07-native-analysis.json`. `verify_saved_geometry.py` checks the protected input copper, all retained comparison copper, every newly planned comparison segment/via and all 33 local models per board. Deliberate surface short, inner-layer separation breach and same-net return shortcut controls are detected. ERC is zero, and an independent fresh netlist/baseline intent check is retained. Existing ignored-check policy, project settings and numeric 3/10/0.25/0.5/0.3 mm limits are inherited unchanged; no new exclusion or severity relaxation was added.

The first pinned Freerouting2.5 native executable failed with a serialization error; it did not route successfully. The separately pinned same-release JVM engine produced a partial two-layer session, subsequently imported into native KiCad and completed with bounded shape-aware additions. Exit0 was never treated as proof of completion. `routing/` retains the actual failure/DSN/SES/execution evidence. This is an experimental adapter, not a qualified toolkit router. The four-layer candidate is an independent bounded geometric rebuild, not an engine claim. Some planner failures are conservative grid/order failures, not proof of physical infeasibility. Rejected R15/R7 moves and an invalid DRV offset via were not adopted.

Earlier C05/C06 electrical, fault, transformer forming/material, finished isolation, via/land assembly, thermal and fit findings remain open at their original scopes; see immutable `../pcb-return-06/acceptance-ledger.json` and linked construction/fault evidence.
