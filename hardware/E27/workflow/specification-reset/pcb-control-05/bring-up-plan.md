# Proposed controlled verification plan — no powered test authorized or performed

Owner basis decision permits design work/publication, not energization. Responsible qualified test person, exact assembled PCB/winding/enclosure/cabling revisions, adopted stackup/process and calibrated instrument/probe ratings are unassigned inputs. They must be recorded before the relevant test. Product insulation and current/thermal stop margins are not established by this plan.

Before power: dimensionally inspect forming, full T1pin pattern/holes/annuli/fillets, barriers/core/yoke/lead exits and clearances against confirmed construction. Check winding ratio/polarity/inductance/DCR/leakage and continuity/shorts with specified instruments; acceptance limits must be frozen. The oval coupon is a mechanical/insertion study, not an approved substitute or a powered safety test. Missing dielectric/thermal/fixture limits stop energization.

Capture VDD and UVLOon/off through cold/hot startup, auxiliary takeover and complete fault/retry cycles using suitable isolated/differential probes. Record207/230/253Vac49–51Hz and adopted temperature/load corners; repeated healthy restart is a failure. VDDrecommended9–35V and source UVLOon19–23/off7.7–8.45 are comparison data, not proof of dynamic behavior. Stop above35V or on growing current/loss of reset; agree stricter design margins before testing. Observe whether auxiliary takeover fits the conditional2.89ms budget; do not report that calculation as a measurement.

Capture actual VGS/VDS and shunt/CS relative to U1GND with correct reference/common-mode ratings and sufficient bandwidth. Characterize total CS-threshold-to-current-turnoff TD over actual driver/gate/load/corners, then finalize R6 from the source equation and recheck current tolerance. Extract actual CS noise, reference bounce and clamp/reset timing. Manufacturer absolute maxima are not design targets; approved VDS/VGS/current/temperature margins and stop limits are required before testing, not invented afterward.

Apply open/short output and NTCopen/short plus defined sense/feedback faults only in a current-limited controlled fixture after its fault energy and expected behavior are reviewed. Bind actual results and any ECO/rework to source hashes and re-run affected graph/ERC/nativeDRC/reviews. This is a test plan only, not test evidence or permission to order boards.
