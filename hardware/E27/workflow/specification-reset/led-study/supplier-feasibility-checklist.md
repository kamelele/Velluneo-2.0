# JLCPCB first-sample feasibility questionnaire

Prepared 2026-11-14 for owner review. NOT SENT. No files uploaded and no quote/order requested. Product data below is preliminary; it is not a manufacturing package.

Goal: receive populated PCB samples for a repairable E27 lamp. One LED colour is sufficient for the initial sample. A separate isolated driver PCB and separate LED panel are contemplated. Final quantities depend on the service minimum; the product goal is one working integrated prototype, not necessarily ordering a single PCB.

1. Can the exact 4000 K LED JB2835BWT-G-H40GA0000-N0000001 (catalog C17218060) be sourced and assembled? Confirm available quantity, MOQ, lead time and exact manufacturer/order code; do not silently substitute another family or CRI.
2. Which flux, voltage and chromaticity bins can be supplied, including an upper flux bound? Can reel/lot/bin identity be retained with the delivered samples?
3. Can you supply fabrication AND SMT population for a single-sided aluminium-core LED panel in one supported order? Identify the actual PCBA service. If not, state what substrate/population combinations you can support.
4. Provide the proposed stackup, dielectric thermal properties and thickness, copper, mask and lead-free finish options, with process evidence relevant to thermal/insulation review. No stackup is already approved.
5. What are the minimum panel/carrier dimensions, tooling rails/fiducials and separation process for a small round final panel? Confirm usable final-board geometry and depanelization constraints; outline and diameter are still under development.
6. Confirm LED land-pattern/polarity review, solder paste/stencil and reflow process compatibility with the exact LED and substrate; identify inspection and rework scope.
7. Can the driver PCB include supplier-sourced/custom transformer and mixed SMD/THT parts? Describe drawing/specification, lead time and incoming verification needed for the transformer. No transformer part or construction is selected yet.
8. What routes exist for a controller not in public stock: global sourcing, pre-order, new-part request or consignment? Identify exact service limitations, MOQ, additional fees and usable-inventory timing.
9. Can the two boards use different order/process routes? Identify minimum populated quantities, setup/stencil fees and manual assembly exclusions. Quote only against a later complete reviewed BOM/geometry; do not infer a price from this questionnaire.
10. Which checks are included: bare-PCB electrical test, AOI, polarity checks and populated-board inspection? Functional mains operation, optical acceptance and safety qualification are not presumed included.
11. Can traceability include PCB revision, exact assembled BOM/DNP, component substitutions authorized in advance, and available lot records?
12. State missing inputs required to confirm feasibility. Do not start production or purchase parts based on this questionnaire.

The owner must explicitly authorize sending this request, choose the recipient/channel and approve any design disclosure. Nothing in this file grants ordering/manufacturing authorization. Retain the supplier's actual response as evidence before closing the corresponding process unknowns.
