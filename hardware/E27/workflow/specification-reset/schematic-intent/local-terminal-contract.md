# Project-defined terminal and wiring conventions

These conventions are explicit engineering assignments for parts whose native drawings use polarity/function rather than numeric pin numbers; they are not claims of vendor numbering. Diodes1=K,2=A; polarized capacitors1=+,2=−; resistors/unpolarized capacitors1/2 interchangeable. Q1 native pins1=G,2=D,3=S,4=exposed drain tab; both drain pins must be connected. U1 uses functional-table1..7 and the omitted physical lead position described in pin-basis-primary.md; package appendix geometric8 differs from functionalHV7.

J1 and J2 are project-defined two-terminal insulated-wire solder-pad interfaces, not a selected proprietary connector. J1.1/J1.2 connect the E27 cap conductors; both are treated as potentially live regardless of socket polarity. J2.1=OUT+,J2.2=OUT− connect internal insulated wires to the future keyed/shrouded panel dock. J2 is not the user mating mechanism, does not impose a commercial connector or a cycle rating, and must remain behind the base cover. Panel-dock geometry/contact retention remains due before placement. The load envelope18–27 V/~250 mA is a candidate analytical envelope; final current accuracy, LED selection, fault voltage and transient protection remain due before schematic acceptance. This draft does not declare a verified electrical interface.

T1 is a custom120:30:22 flyback winding on the exact TDK raw bobbin. Pin numbers/polarity and explicit unused pins follow pin-basis-primary.md. It is not a stocked finished transformer; insulation, winding order, leakage, manufacturability and phase/withstand validation remain open. No physical insulation dimension is invented from the two-row pin arrangement.

F1→RIN→BR1 AC input limits one cap conductor; the other connects to BR1's second AC input. Fuse interruption does not establish a safely isolated accessible interior. Primary return and output return remain electrically separate. No PE,Y capacitor or feedback optocoupler is fitted in this proposed graph.
