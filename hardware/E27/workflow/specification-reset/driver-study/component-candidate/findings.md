## Actual calculations

- **2592 normal averaged witnesses** refreshed for 2.304 mH: 33.20–82.00 kHz; normal linear flux 0.093–0.128 T using Amin. Previous EFD20 witness maximum was 0.186 T. This is the same energy/current witness convention, not an executable UCC controller model.
- At the 1.6 V backup current threshold, high L and low sense resistance, plus an assumed extra 430 ns input-voltage ramp, the EFD25 linear flux proxy is **0.275 T**. Previous EFD20 proxy was 0.368 T even before delay. This is a useful static improvement, **not** a bound on saturation, fault reset, repeated pulses or survival. Residual flux and nonlinear magnetics remain unresolved.
- Conservative secondary RMS upper bound is 0.519 A. Applying the manufacturer's frequency correction at each constructed normal frequency, the 63 V output part reaches at most **69.2%** of rated ripple allowance; the smaller 50 V part reaches 92.2%. No low-frequency burst/line ripple or actual temperature/lifetime qualification is implied. ESR is specified at 100 kHz; 0.12/0.24 ohm at the actual waveform are sensitivities, not guaranteed ESR over temperature/frequency.
- MOSFET conduction loss is 0.045–0.127 W using the 25°C resistance limit; an assumed 2.5× hot-resistance multiplier gives up to 0.316 W. Switching/Coss/overlap/clamp/avalanche losses and local heat spreading are still required. The datasheet 38°C/W thermal value requires a 1-inch² 2-oz copper test board and is **not** adopted for this bulb.
- **16 periodic switching/storage witnesses** couple a constructed triangular delivered current to the pinned Cree static 25°C diode law, 7/8 LEDs, four capacitances and two assumed ESRs. Maximum LED current is about **0.298 A** in these nominal-control witnesses, not a worst-controller-corner bound. Largest capacitor RMS is about 0.362 A. Half-step refinement changes peak current by less than 0.04 mA; mean-current charge balance is checked. These do not establish PstLM/SVM, colour/flux, thermal current or actual controller ripple.

## Corrected drain-voltage selection

Independent review identified that the initially explored STN1NK60Z (600 V) fails the static high-line/open-output screen: 357.8 V bus + four times the refreshed OVP endpoint plus 1.2 V diode drop + an assumed 100 V overshoot is approximately **609 V**. It is rejected for this proposal. **STN1NK80Z (800 V)** is substituted, and conduction and gate-charge wording are refreshed against its native DS14259 rev2 July2026 source. The same illustrative stress leaves approximately 191 V to the absolute rating, but 100 V overshoot is still an assumption, OVP is static/25°C and surge/latency are unbounded. This repairs the inadequate illustrative rating choice; it does not qualify a clamp or close the actual drain/SOA/transient assessment. Never treat single-pulse avalanche capability as continuous clamp protection.

## Startup: narrowed component gap, remaining major finding

The original 8–12 uF output-capacitor interval is only initial room-temperature tolerance. Panasonic allows ±30% capacitance after endurance and ±10% after solder heat. Multiplying those separate limits as an intentionally conservative sensitivity gives **5.04–17.16 uF** before temperature; adding an analyst ±20% in-operation temperature sensitivity gives **4.032–20.592 uF**. Their product is not a manufacturer-guaranteed combined limit or a service-life model.

With IRUN max 2.65 mA, assumed gate charge ≤12 nC at 106 kHz and a separate 0.3 mA allowance, total draw is 4.222 mA, rounded to a **conditional 4.3 mA budget**. The older 6 mA case is retained in results; it is not silently removed. The 12 nC gate allowance, auxiliary delivery and other draw still need supporting actual-device/waveform evidence.

At the 20.592 uF sensitivity, minimum VDD storage, 19 V turn-on, and **assumed 0.300 ms initial acquisition**, the average-current takeover calculation gives approximately **10.82 V**, above the 10 V internal calculation target. At 1 ms acquisition the same assumptions fail. **0.300 ms is an acceptance hypothesis, not a sourced bound on the IC's first three cycles.** The initial-cycle and real-load conditions of major RC-BIAS-001 remain open. A negative extrapolated capacitor voltage in a failed sensitivity is merely model failure after UVLO would have occurred; it is not a prediction of hardware below ground.

Auxiliary/CV/25°C OVP sensitivity endpoints are recalculated for 22/30 auxiliary turns in results.json; the old endpoints must not be reused as new-transformer bounds. CV temperature slope remains nominal, and all diode/loss/ripple assumptions remain as in the parent. At no panel, intended operation is non-destructive CV regulation with OVP backup. No user-access voltage/energy or waiting time is selected.
