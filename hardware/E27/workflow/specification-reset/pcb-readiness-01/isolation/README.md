Change the proposed tape allocation to **no optional base holding wrap, two complete 1350F-1 wraps between primary and auxiliary, one holding wrap between auxiliary and TIW secondary, one outer holding wrap**. Four total tape wraps preserve the existing2.293mm worst seam envelope. The bobbin may replace the omitted base holding tape as enamel-to-core ground insulation only after all-path wall/material/function acceptance meets the selected system's0.3mm PM-9630 entry. Two P/A wraps satisfy this selected system's 1350F-1 interwinding layer entry; the earlier one-wrap recipe cannot be represented as satisfying that entry. This does not imply every possible insulation system requires the same layer count.

No actual leakage, capacitance, AL or gap-fringing-loss invariance is claimed. The prior full-shell nominal collision result is retained only for the unchanged outer envelope; cut lengths/hot DCR remain construction budgets, not measured parameters. Finished Lp/polarity/DCR/leakage and thermal behavior require their actual later acceptance evidence.

## Mandatory finished construction and PCB handoff

The manufacturing drawing must preserve dot starts1/2/6 and finishes5/4/10. Pins3/7/8/9 remain unwound but physically present. Primary and auxiliary conductors/loops stay at row1–5, secondary terminations at row6–10. Do not route a primary lead through the secondary terminal pocket or allow an NC pin/copper island to bridge the separation region. Retain TIW insulation continuously through the winding and the exit route, with stripped/tinned zones confined to defined secondary terminal wells; no insulation nick, burned-back zone or solder splash is allowed to become an unassessed primary-to-secondary path.

Treat core/yokes as hazardous primary-domain objects for accessibility/separation analysis until their actual insulation construction is established. Floating metal is not an insulation barrier. The20.1mm pad-row result says nothing about ferrite/yoke proximity to a bare secondary terminal above the board. A dimensioned section must show those nearest three-dimensional air/surface/solid paths under actual winding, sleeving, molding, solder and assembly tolerances. The provisional raw-bobbin25.7x26.5mm courtyard is not a finished transformer envelope. **30x30x18mm** is a proposed maximum finished-interface allocation, to be demonstrated on the complete drawing before adoption; it is not a measured/guaranteed size.

The source-aware termination route separates TIW end preparation from the molded bobbin. TEX-E0.30 catalogue solderability is tested at420°C/4s; specific bobbin heat-resistance evidence is350°C/3.5s and TDK's generic processing example400°C/1s. None establishes a shared qualified production profile. Propose off-bobbin end preparation and then a separately qualified pin-joint process, with controlled insulation retreat, wetting, flux residue and pin strain. Do not apply420°C/4s to the assembled bobbin by copying a wire-only test. These are proposed process requirements, not authorization to perform a physical test.
