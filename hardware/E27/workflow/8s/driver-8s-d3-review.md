# 8S-D3 control review and corrections

Codex owns E27-8S-CAD-CONTROL for this follow-up. The reviewed input was 8S-D2 commit `cff8aa2640a1573fba135b6044f90b9f35f552bf`. The [native D3 schematic](../../outputs/Zasilacz_8S_Draft/Zasilacz_8S_Draft.kicad_sch) and [control pin map](driver-8s-control-input.json) contain the resulting changes. The schematic gate remains open.

## Changes captured

- **Timer input.** U108 SN74LVC1G17DBVR regenerates PERMIT_RETURN locally before U105 MR. S1 and TH1 still physically interrupt the enable feed, and S2 independently opens LED+. At the 3 V logic datasheet test point, screening a 2.4 V loaded permit output gives about 2.35 V at PERMIT_RETURN before contact losses, versus the buffer's 1.92 V maximum rising threshold at that test point. With the timer input as its light load, the buffer can supply near its own rail. Check switch resistance, supply corners and the slow intermediate-input region. An intermediate permit can still satisfy SHDN before it qualifies the timer. Include this condition in the bench sweep of contact resistance and slow ramps. [TI buffer](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/sn74lvc1g17.pdf) · [TI timer](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps3840.pdf) · [JLCPCB C7836](https://jlcpcb.com/partdetail/C7836)
- **Pull-up headroom.** R31, R32 and R37 are now 33 kΩ. U103 uses one signal input per inverter and ties the second input to V3V3_HOLD. With 20 µA input leakage, up to 0.30 µA open-drain leakage and a +1% pull-up, the screening drop is 0.677 V, leaving about 2.56 V at a 3.234 V rail. Compare with the 2.0 V maximum rising threshold at the specified 3 V and 3.6 V test points. A low output draws up to about 103 µA per pull-up at 3.366 V and −1%. This closes the identified first-order high-level deficit. It also invalidates the D2 hold-current estimate as a guaranteed bound. Include extra CMOS supply current in the intermediate input region and leakage at temperature. [Nexperia 74LVC132A](https://assets.nexperia.com/documents/data-sheet/74LVC132A.pdf) · [TI TPS3700](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps3700.pdf)
- **Hold supply.** C14 is now a Panasonic EEHZE1H101V candidate, 100 µF, 50 V, 10 mm by 10.5 mm. Initial −20% tolerance and an additional −30% endurance drift screen to 56 µF at the datasheet reference conditions before temperature and solder allowances. The 68 µF option would screen to 38.1 µF. With 56 µF and a 20-to-4 V hold swing, retention is 2.99 s at an assumed 300 µA or 2.24 s at 400 µA. These are sensitivity points rather than a measured or worst-case current budget. C17 is a 50 V Murata 10 µF candidate. At least 2.2 µF effective is required at the LDO output. [Panasonic ZE](https://industrial.panasonic.com/cdbs/www-data/pdf/RDD0000/ABA0000C1236.pdf) · [JLCPCB C454352](https://jlcpcb.com/partdetail/PANASONIC-EEHZE1H101V/C454352) · [JLCPCB C77102](https://jlcpcb.com/partdetail/C77102)
- **Charging pulse.** R24 is a pulse-rated Vishay CRCW12061K00FKECHP candidate in 1206. An ideal 27 V step into the empty hold capacitor screens to 0.74 W initial at the resistor low corner and 44 mJ total for 120 µF at the capacitor high corner. Verify the repetitive pulse curve, temperature derating and measured waveform before release. [Vishay CRCW-HP e3](https://www.vishay.com/docs/20043/crcwhpe3.pdf) · [JLCPCB C4133334](https://jlcpcb.com/partdetail/VishayIntertech-CRCW12061K00FKECHP/C4133334)
- **Fresh evidence.** The verification command now exports the active schematic and runs ERC itself, checks the source path and tests that the schematic hash stays unchanged. It verifies the changed timer pins, resistor values and selected footprints. KiCad 10.0.8 exported 90 components, 252 connected pins and 18 explicit no-connects. ERC gives 0 errors and the retained warning about the intentional SR-to-SEC_GND strap. The Boolean sequence model checks the same ideal state boundaries as D2. ## Findings before layout

- **Startup timing and brownout.** With 39 nF ±10% effective and a discharged CT, the TI timing extremes screen to about 12.6–37.6 ms before the intrinsic delay. Measure loaded startup, short energy and incomplete CT discharge with contact bounce. Add or demonstrate a direct hardware inhibit for the below-spec logic-supply window while RAW can still power the eFuse. Verify preset/clear overlap and the U101 pass-short fault. [TI TPS3840](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps3840.pdf) · [TI SN74LVC74A](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/sn74lvc74a.pdf)
- **Fresh enable and reset policy.** The D3 readiness latch can retain its qualification through a later S1 reclosure or RAW recovery. Requalify reserve energy and valid RAW on each fresh start, or establish acceptable repeat-pulse energy with a bounded retry policy. Loss of the held rail with mains still present can erase the fault latch. A mains-only reset would require a separate way to establish that mains was removed.
- **Interfaces.** Check J2 connector cross-shorts, especially permit-return pin 2 to FUSED pin 3 and an S2 bypass across pins 3 and 4. Choose the physical switch and connector hardware with the required sequence, isolation and contact wetting. T1 remains a supplier drawing/RFQ item. Confirm the cold 8S voltage and final electrical bounds. Complete the approved physical connectivity comparison and schematic review before formal placement.

## Review provenance
