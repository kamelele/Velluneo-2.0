# E27-8S-IFACE — schematic preparation

The specification decision and fabrication proposal are recorded in [the owner decision](process-decision.md). Codex owns this preparation task. Inputs are the 8S specification, the approved process review, the [output protection study](output-protection-study.md), Rev K for the flyback and Rev H for the existing interface. The task delivers a candidate connector map, a voltage window and explicit conditions for schematic review.

## Proposed electrical interface

- Driver J_LED pin 1 to insert J_LED pin 1 is LED+. Driver J_LED pin 2 to insert J_LED pin 2 is LED−, the return for the only LED current path. The legacy J3-3 second cathode goes out of the electrical map. Use these logical and proposed pin assignments for the new two-position connection. Confirm physical keying and mating access before the pin intent is accepted.
- Retain the Rev H J2 convention as the reference for the independent switches. J2-1 feeds S1 (normally open) and TH1 (normally closed) in series to J2-2. J2-3 connects to J2-4 through S2 (normally open). S2 remains in the positive LED feed. The driver may arm only after the LED contacts mate and the S1 and TH1 path permits drive. On removal, S1 disables drive first, S2 opens the feed next, then the contacts become accessible. Confirm the actual sequence and wiring with the housing design. [Rev H schematic](../../outputs/Zasilacz_RevH_Sterowanie/Zasilacz_RevH_Sterowanie.kicad_sch)
- Reserve dedicated connections for the sensing resistor at the driver. Keep the 8S return, contact-side discharge and voltage feedback references explicit. Check the circuit for bypass paths through the return, clamp and protection networks. For the insert, the independent net intent starts from the selected LED datasheet and requires exactly one path through eight anode-to-cathode junctions.

## Candidate driver settings and limits

- Use 250–280 mA for bench characterization. A 0.130 Ω, 1% sense resistor gives about 276 mA nominal and 268–285 mA at the datasheet 25°C comparator corners. A 0.137 Ω, 1% option gives about 262 mA nominal and 254–270 mA. Keep both options available for review. Final bin and current selection depends on optical and thermal evidence.
- Rev K uses 110 kΩ and 5.62 kΩ, both 0.1%, for 26.02 V nominal unloaded voltage regulation. Applying the LYTSwitch-6 1.250–1.280 V feedback threshold at 25°C and the initial resistor tolerances gives **25.67–26.38 V**. The low corner is about 0.43 V below the 26.10 V cold-load screening target in the protection study.
- A 113 kΩ / 5.62 kΩ candidate gives **26.33–27.07 V** at those same corners and 26.70 V nominal. The two available static margins are about **0.23 V** above the 26.10 V screening target and **0.47 V** below the earliest external eFuse trip. Re-evaluate these margins for temperature, feedback bias, line/load changes, ripple and transients. Keep the separate VO supply arrangement under review because the upper static raw-output corner is above the LYTSwitch-6 27 V absolute maximum at VO.
- Use the 27.54–28.99 V external eFuse trip range as a candidate. The nominal current regulator controls the converter's energy delivery. The output reservoir can already hold energy before the eFuse reconnects. Capture the first current pulse and its duration during insertion and after interruption with the reservoir precharged. Evaluate the eFuse ramp, placement of the bulk capacitance upstream of S2, and a contact-side discharge path against the measured load.
