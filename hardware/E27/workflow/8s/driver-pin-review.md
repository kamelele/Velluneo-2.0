Keep SEC_GND and LED_RTN joined through the sense path only, including the intentional clamp across R9. The InSOP footprint description says 17 leads, while the manufacturer drawing shows 20 physical leads. Verify the actual geometry and pads against the drawing before using that footprint. - **U101 TPS7A1633DGNR, DGN.** Pins 5 EN and 8 IN → RAW; 1 OUT → 3V3_PERMIT; 4 GND and pad 9 → LED_RTN in the preferred candidate. This changes the previous proposal to avoid placing the regulator and capacitor return currents on the eFuse internal RTN reference. Pin 2 is DNC on the fixed-voltage device. The 1 kΩ series resistor and 10 kΩ pull-down set a nominal ~3.0 V SHDN high when the loop is closed. Return the SHDN pull-down to LED_RTN. A passive implementation still needs leakage, transient, contact and short-latch checks. Check SHDN at both references through start-up and input transients, including regulator pass short. Use an output capacitor rated for the credible fault voltage after derating, or bound that voltage independently. Keep eFuse mode, ramp, OVP and ILIM networks on EF_RTN. Confirm dry-contact operation of S1 and TH1 at that current and the lowest voltage. ## Voltage window and protection behavior

The following are calculated screening conditions. They use the 25°C flyback CV limits 26.33–27.07 V and the previously calculated eFuse limits. Use `delta = V(EF_RTN) − V(LED_RTN)` as a signed input. Characterize it with the actual control network connected.

- **Nuisance trip:** ignoring the small sense drop for conservative no-load screening, the lowest estimated eFuse rising threshold minus maximum CV is 27.54 − 27.07 = **0.47 V**, before reference offset and ripple. The design condition is `V(RAW relative to LED_RTN, normal peak) < 27.54 V + delta_min`, with margin for tolerances.
- **Contact voltage:** high static trip is 28.99 V relative to EF_RTN. Reserve **1.01 V minus delta_max** for dynamic effects, uncertainty and any required margin to 30 V, before crediting series drops. The TI +0.3 V RTN-to-GND absolute-maximum limit is a stress rating. An open top resistor or shorted bottom resistor can select the factory 31–34 V OVP, so these divider faults require the independent flyback bound to hold. As a sensitivity point only, +0.30 V leaves about **0.71 V**. The datasheet’s typical OVP timing is to FLT. Verify actual peak and FET turn-off independently on RAW, FUSED and both contacts.
- **Recovery:** the falling threshold can be 25.53–26.62 V relative to EF_RTN. At delta = 0, a 27.07 V no-load bus can remain above the highest rearm threshold by 0.45 V and above the lowest by 1.54 V. Cycling SHDN clears an overload latch, while recovery from OVP still requires RAW to fall below the falling threshold. Treat mains removal and restoration after a genuine trip as a candidate recovery method. Verify that normal insertion and all input ramps avoid nuisance trips. If automatic recovery is required, the present CV/OVP pairing needs a controlled reset of RAW or another protection topology.
- **Discharge:** with 4.7 kΩ +1%, 10 µF and no leakage, the 30-to-5 V decay takes **85.1 ms** after isolation, leaving **14.9 ms** for switch and circuit delays. At 30 V the resistor dissipates up to **0.194 W** at −1%. A 10 kΩ ±1% alternative with ≤4.7 µF gives **85.1 ms** and ≤0.091 W. Prefer the lower-loss option only after total accessible-side capacitance, leakage and measured timing justify it. In a stuck-S2 test include the eFuse OUT bypass, and capture whether opening S1 still removes energy. The reservoir upstream stores about **0.168 J** at 470 µF and 26.70 V. Test the pulse under normal hot insertion and separately assess the shorted-eFuse case with S2 operating. [TI TPS2660x, electrical and timing characteristics](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps2660.pdf) · [Prior protection study](output-protection-study.md)

## Corrections from the baseline review

- **D8 polarity.** The previous proposal reversed the archived Rev K clamp. For the positive short transient, the candidate preserves Rev K polarity: D8 anode to LED_RTN and cathode to SEC_GND. The Power Integrations diode example uses the same positive-transient clamp function. The forward drop of a diode alone cannot establish compliance with all IS limits. Capture both polarities, including the energy from C6. The 27 V absolute maximum at VO also requires checking the pass regulator, including its shorted condition. The internal VO bleed acts through that regulator and cannot be credited as a guaranteed RAW bleed. [Power Integrations, Fig. 10 and absolute maximum ratings](https://www.power.com/sites/default/files/documents/lytswitch-6_family_datasheet.pdf#page=10)
- **Short-circuit latch.** The 402 kΩ mode selects active current limiting with latch-off on eFuse thermal shutdown. The 30.1 kΩ ILIM sets roughly 0.40 A nominal, above the flyback's ~0.285 A high corner. A sustained current-limited fault may never thermally latch U2, so eFuse alone does not carry forward the old comparator/latch behavior. Keep an explicit short-circuit detection and reset function in the design budget. Evaluate a single output voltage monitor with start-up blanking and a latch driving SHDN. It must cover a hard short present at enable, avoid a false trip during the ramp, and retain safe state during a RAW collapse. Set the monitor threshold only after separating the short-fault and minimum healthy output envelopes. Verify partial shorts and open string separately. Healthy 8S and faulted 7S string voltage distributions can overlap, so a partial-short voltage threshold needs evidence before it can be credited. [TI TPS2660x operating modes](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps2660.pdf) · [Rev H controller baseline](../../outputs/Zasilacz_RevH_Sterowanie/README.md)
