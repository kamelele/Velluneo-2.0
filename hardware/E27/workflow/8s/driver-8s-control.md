Task E27-8S-CAD-CONTROL is owned by Codex. Input is the owner-authorized 8S-D1 draft at `5a20a9c7f85fb8baec4ab6956e26f457207b724b`, plus the [short-protection study](short-protection.md) and the physical pin data cited below. Output is a revision of the existing [native 8S schematic](../../outputs/Zasilacz_8S_Draft/Zasilacz_8S_Draft.kicad_sch), with independent approval of the full pin contract still pending. The task does not advance the schematic gate.

## Circuit

- U104 TPS3840PL30 gives an active-low power reset on the held 3.3 V supply. U106 SN74LVC74A uses the asynchronous preset and clear pins of its two storage elements. The first remembers that the hold rail reached the threshold. The second remembers a qualified short fault. Both clear when the held rail falls below the reset threshold. The fault memory stays set through S1 toggling and while the held rail remains valid. Verify brief preset/clear overlap during power-down, a clean POR on recovery and the output state while supply is below logic operating limits. [TI TPS3840](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps3840.pdf) · [TI SN74LVC74A](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/sn74lvc74a.pdf)
- U107 drives the permit feed when POR is clear, readiness has been seen and no fault is latched. S1 and TH1 remain physically in series between this feed and SHDN. R18 and R34 hold their respective nodes low with the loop open. S2 independently interrupts LED+. A temporary hold-ready signal loss after start does not reset either memory. Check the valid logic-high margin at U105 MR with the loop loaded, contact wetting and cable transients. [TI SN74LVC1G11](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/sn74lvc1g11.pdf) · [TI TPS2660x](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps2660.pdf)
- U105 TPS3840DL29 starts the blanking interval from the closed permit return. A 39 nF timing capacitor gives about 24 ms typical using TI's typical 619 ms/µF. Screen the full timing spread and choose the final capacitor against loaded ramp time and fault energy. U103 uses the Nexperia 74LVC132APW,118 Schmitt NAND with slow-input support. R38 and C18 give a nominal 1 ms time constant on the short signal. The actual time to cross the threshold depends on the logic swing and threshold. It is not a fixed 1 ms timer. A sustained low present at the end of blanking can set the fault immediately because the filter runs during startup. [Nexperia 74LVC132A](https://assets.nexperia.com/documents/data-sheet/74LVC132A.pdf) · [JLCPCB C548102](https://jlcpcb.com/partdetail/Nexperia-74LVC132APW118/C548102) · [JLCPCB C2066767](https://jlcpcb.com/partdetail/TexasInstruments-TPS3840PL30DBVR/C2066767) · [JLCPCB C2066757](https://jlcpcb.com/partdetail/TexasInstruments-TPS3840DL29DBVR/C2066757)
- C14 increases from 47 to 68 µF nominal to accommodate the additional control current. With 47 µF effective, 250 µA in the latched condition and a 20 to 4 V hold swing, the screening time is 3.01 s. The permit-loop load is separate from this static estimate and consumes hold charge until the fault has latched. Measure the actual current, earliest POR, complete retention and recharge. Verify if a dropout under live mains can outlast the hold interval. [Previous retention and bench plan](short-protection.md#retention-and-startup-sizing)

## Verification

The KiCad export matched the provisional maps. The digital boundary model exercised supply not ready, cold-start short, loss of hold-ready indication, hard short after blanking, opening and reclosing S1 with a stored fault, and reset after POR. It does not simulate component tolerances, propagation races, regulator faults or transient waveforms. The timing and fault tests still apply.

ERC power-source annotations describe actual sources. RAW and SEC_GND come from the transformer and secondary rectifier, LED_RTN is their return through R9, HOLD_RAW is fed through D10, VO is fed by Q1, and the BPP and BPS supplies come from the controller and its bias paths. The one ERC warning reflects the documented SR grounding for diode rectification. The pin also senses its strap during startup. The warning is left active. [Power Integrations LYTSwitch-6](https://www.power.com/sites/default/files/documents/lytswitch-6_family_datasheet.pdf)
