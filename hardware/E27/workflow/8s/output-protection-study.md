# 8S output protection and cold-start design study

This specification study supports `OPEN-CC` for the 8S driver. Codex prepared it from the [revision 2 specification](specification.md), the archived Rev K and Rev H design data, and the manufacturer datasheets. The work scope is calculation and test planning. The proposed values are starting points for schematic review.

## Voltage window

Rev H already uses the TPS26600PWPR and a 226 kΩ / 10 kΩ OVP divider with 0.1% resistors. Reuse is a candidate for the single-board design. For a divider from IN through Rtop to OVP and Rbottom to RTN, the input trip is `Vref × (1 + Rtop / Rbottom) + Ipin × Rtop`. Use the TI reference range of 1.170–1.225 V rising, 1.085–1.125 V falling and ±100 nA pin leakage.

- Calculated rising trip is **27.54–28.99 V**, with 28.08 V typical. The high corner leaves **1.01 V** for all dynamic excursions and measurement uncertainty up to the 30 V contact limit.
- Calculated falling rearm is **25.53–26.62 V**, with 25.96 V typical. Plan recovery after an overvoltage or open string so the raw output can fall below the rearm threshold before reconnecting. Verify that the constant-current loop and the overvoltage disconnect recover without repetitive start attempts.
- Using the previous unverified **25.6 V** cold-string screen plus an illustrative **0.50 V** for wiring, eFuse, sensing and ripple gives **26.10 V** required at the source. This leaves **1.44 V** to the earliest eFuse trip. As a first bench target, a normal raw-output peak no higher than 27.0 V would leave about 0.54 V to that earliest trip. Derive the actual minimum and maximum normal output envelope from measured cold string voltage and flyback tolerances.
- The corner calculation includes initial resistor tolerance and the datasheet comparator and leakage ranges. Temperature tracking and ageing of the resistors, switching spikes, source impedance, layout and turn-off dynamics require separate evaluation. The 6 µs OVP timing listed by TI is a typical time to FLT under a specified test condition. Capture actual contact voltage during trip. The internal fallback OVP can reach **34 V**, so a fault in the external divider needs an independent normal flyback voltage limit.

## Cold string and removal

- At 260–280 mA the Cree graph shows roughly 2.93–2.97 V per LED at 25°C, or about 23.4–23.8 V for eight typical units. Applying the datasheet typical temperature coefficient of −0.9 mV/°C to all eight adds about 0.18 V for cooling from 25°C to 0°C. This sensitivity estimate uses the typical curve. The manufacturer's maximum forward voltage table is at 55 mA. Measure the cold 8S voltage at the maximum expected current, including current tolerance, using the selected bins and an agreed minimum start temperature. [Cree datasheet, pp. 4 and 6](https://downloads.cree-led.com/files/ds/j/JSeries-2835-3V-Consolidated.pdf#page=4)
- Proposed functional path is flyback raw output and reservoir → independent eFuse cutoff → S2 physical opening of LED+ → contacts → 8S insert → sensed return. S1 and TH1 inhibit energization. Confirm the reservoir is upstream of S2 and review whether current from a precharged reservoir can bypass regulation momentarily at insertion. Verify insertion peak current independently of steady-state ISENSE. Confirm the contact-side capacitance and clearance of the S2 open path before assigning component values and physical pins.
- For a simple passive discharge path from 30 V to 5 V in 100 ms, `Rmax = 0.1 / (Cmax × ln(30/5))`. For **10 µF maximum** on the exposed side, the total discharge-path resistance must be below about **5.58 kΩ**. A 4.7 kΩ ±1% path would give about **85 ms** and dissipate about **0.19 W** at 30 V. For **47 µF maximum** the limit drops to about **1.19 kΩ**. Keep this sensitivity analysis conditional until actual capacitance, power budget and discharge topology are known.
- Qualification should cover loss of flyback feedback with the independent cutoff operating, cutoff or divider fault with the normal voltage limit operating, a stuck S2 with S1 and TH1 healthy, and loss of the shutdown path with S2 opening. Also capture unloaded start, open and shorted string, and insertion with the reservoir precharged. Record peak voltage and the time from normal opening to below 5 V at the accessible contacts.

The next evidence is a measured cold-string envelope and a connector and interlock mapping for both ends. This study establishes candidate values and identifies remaining conditions. It is separate from the [reviewed revision 2 packet](stage-packet.md) and the measurements required by [the product requirements](requirements.json).
