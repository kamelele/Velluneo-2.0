## Specific baseline findings

Coordinates below are millimetres from PCB left/top, not native absolute coordinates (which add100mm).

1. **PWR-01 — switching copper used as a heat spreader.** The saved SOURCE zones total264.24mm² across both faces. Actual connectivity places SOURCE between the internal high-side MOSFET and the current-sense network. Large SOURCE pours are consequently high-dv/dt copper, not DC_RETURN. They increase parasitic coupling opportunity. Correction: remove broad SOURCE heat pours, retain deliberate short current/bypass conductors, recalculate copper-area comparisons and withdraw the unsupported150mm²SOURCE cooling target. Post-change U1 temperatures require measurement; do not retain a previous thetaJA prediction.
2. **GEO-05/PWR-02 — C5 return crosses two layers.** C5.1→BP is only2.247mm, but the SOURCE pad is on a different B.Cu filled island from the IC SOURCE pins. Saved polygon/via inspection shows the return must crossB→F→B. One explicit route is14.66mm toU1.7 plus barrels; this is not a measured HF impedance. An initial unindexed `Contains()` conclusion was withdrawn after checking individual subpolygons. Correction: move C5 between the appropriate BP/SOURCE pin ends and provide two direct same-face connections, no bypass-return vias. Compare the complete external-loop proxy, not BP length alone.
3. **GEO-03/PWR-03 — force path detours through the coil terminal.** R5.2→D1.1 uses about14.82mm and two vias, including a visit toT1.2. Pad-centre distance is9.48mm. Correction: route the actual commutation force branch directly and join the coil branch at the diode where possible, while keeping the U1 FB takeoff joined only inside the physical R5 pad. Same-net DRC cannot verify that separation; inspect copper intersections and branch topology.
4. **PWR-04 — output-capacitor terminals are distant from the stage.** C6+→T1+ and C6−→D1 anode centre distances are41.18mm and40.98mm. The horizontal can occupies central space but its terminals stay at the far-left edge. Correction: study a rotated horizontal mounting and coordinated power-stage placement. Keep the same capacitor, maximum22×13mmcan, normal hand-formed leads/support and five-mmfree vent. A vent reserve extending beyond PCB outline remains an external fixture/enclosure requirement, not proof of clearance. Compare actual routed loops after the candidate is connected; do not equate endpoint distance to current-loop area.
5. **GEO-04/PWR-05 — M network spread.** R7→U1M route is19.21mm, totalMtrack23.04mm. Correction: keep the divider and its source-reference path together and shorten/control the M receiver connection, avoiding power-force/capacitive coupling. Floating-source references must remain distinct from DC_RETURN.
6. **GEO-01/02 — routing debris.** Baseline has167tracksegments/11vias;60segments strictly below0.30mm and26at most0.10mm within1nm counting tolerance. There is an exactly duplicated AC_N segment at(31.75,22.5)→(31.8,22.5). Correction: remove duplicates and redundant stubs, merge collinear compatible segments and smooth verified low-frequency paths. A short segment or right-angle bend alone is not proof of an electrical fault; preserving clearance/topology takes priority.
7. **SPATIAL — missing executable contract and conflicting C6 declarations.** Hardware points to no spatial file. The geometry JSON proposes a both-face capacitor-body restriction, while the saved board implements only a front-side copper keepout and has rear parts/traces under the can. Correction: preserve and explicitly implement the conservative both-face body/vent projection in the routing candidate; relocate affected parts/ports. Use independently specified maximum-case geometry, not a rectangle reverse-engineered solely from existing traces.
8. **TOOLKIT integration limits.** The pinned spatial backend supports a four-edge rectangle, while the baseline has eight chamfer segments. Preserve50×32mmouterdimensions and deliberately migrate the prototype outline to four straight edges. Native helper `LoadBoard`/`SaveBoard` requires raw net-identity verification; prepare matching neutral named areas with the raw loader, then preview/apply only generated rules/consumer files. Do not patch the immutable dependency or hand-edit generated DRC rules.
